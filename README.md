# 🎓 Scholarship & Job Portal

A full-stack web platform designed to help students discover **job opportunities, internships, and scholarships** through a centralized and easy-to-use portal.

## 🚀 Overview

The Scholarship & Job Portal provides students with a single platform where they can explore career opportunities and scholarship programs.

The platform is designed with separate access for:

- 👨‍🎓 Students
- 🛡️ Administrators

Students can register and securely sign in using their credentials, while administrators have dedicated access to manage jobs, scholarships, and important portal information.

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Portal

- Student registration and login
- Secure authentication
- Browse available job opportunities
- Browse scholarship opportunities
- View opportunity details
- Search and explore opportunities
- Student-friendly interface
- Responsive design

### 🛡️ Admin Portal

Administrators have a separate portal for managing the platform.

Admin capabilities include:

- Add new job opportunities
- Update job information
- Remove outdated jobs
- Add scholarship opportunities
- Update scholarship information
- Remove scholarship information
- Manage important portal content
- Maintain opportunity information

> 🔐 Administrative features are restricted to authorized administrators.

- **Programming Language:** JavaScript
